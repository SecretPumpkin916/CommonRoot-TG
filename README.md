# TG — A Symbolic System of Root and Consciousness

**All things share a common root. All things are consciousness.**

TG is a minimal, self-consistent formal symbolic system. It starts from a single **Root (K)** and derives everything — existence, nonexistence, the observer, consciousness — through two axioms and ten results.

### The Ten Results

1. ZF explodes under self-referential totality. TG absorbs it via derivatives.
2. The derivative mechanism is defined: K unfolds into &.
3. **Consciousness must exist.** K requires an observer to collapse.
4. The Root-Trace Diagram cannot be drawn — by either path.
5. The Root-Trace Diagram cannot be named — by either path.
6. **Nothing is absolute.** null and unll do not absolutely exist.
7. The observer is not outside the system — the observer is a derivative.
8. All derivatives are observers. **& = Ψ.**
9. All observers are roots. **Ψ = K.**
10. **All is Ψ. All is K.**

## Why This Exists

Most formal systems forbid self-reference or break under totality.  
TG does neither. It starts with totality and shows that what looks like an explosion is just the birth of a derivative.

If you're interested in foundations of mathematics, formal approaches to consciousness, the observer problem, or minimal symbolic systems — this is for you.
